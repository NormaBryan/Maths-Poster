📊 STATISTICS

Collect, organise and interpret data.

Mean = Sum ÷ Count

Example: 4, 6, 8
Mean = 18 ÷ 3 = 6

💯 PERCENTAGES

Percent means "out of 100".

20% of 150 = 30

Calculate: 150 × 0.20 = 30

🎲 PROBABILITY

Measure how likely an event is.

P(E) = Favourable ÷ Total

Rolling a 6 on a fair die:
P(6) = 1/6
